## Description

AuraLunis turns your phone into a living celestial instrument. Explore the Moon, planets, constellations, and satellites — computed in real time for your location and time.

LIVING ASTROLABE
Your home screen is an all-sky dial that plots what's above the horizon right now. Moon phase, visible planets, rise and set times — all live, all real.

SKY LENS
Explore Jupiter, Venus, the Moon, constellations, and more using a sensor-aligned planetarium built around real astronomical positions.

TONIGHT SCORE
A clear viewing-quality number from cloud cover, moon brightness, and your local conditions, so you know whether it's worth going outside.

LEARN
Animated lessons on the solar system, moon phases, constellations, star brightness, and deep-sky objects, each linked back to the live sky.

SATELLITE TRACKER
Real orbital elements propagated with SGP4. See which satellites are above your horizon right now.

PRIVACY FIRST
Location, sensor, and vault data stay on your device except approximate coordinates used for the weather forecast. Your Vault is encrypted.
